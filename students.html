Getting Started with BoodleBox

A student's guide to what BoodleBox is and how to use it without letting it think for you.

What BoodleBox is

BoodleBox is a web-based AI workspace your college provides so you can use several major AI models — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and others — through one account, instead of juggling separate personal subscriptions. It's built for education, with institutional data-privacy protections that a personal AI account doesn't have — which is why it's the default place to put real course materials.

It also lets you build reusable custom bots: a saved chat, pointed at specific documents, with instructions that make it behave consistently every time you return to it.

BoodleBox is where you're free to experiment and lean on AI heavily while you're learning — because the graded work that actually shows what you know is designed separately, in ways AI can't do for you.

First-time setup

  1. Sign in with your college account at your institution's BoodleBox link (ask your instructor or campus IT if you don't have it).
  2. Take the platform's own interactive getting-started tour if one is offered on first login.
  3. Note the two icons you'll use constantly: the brain icon 🧠 (your Knowledge Bank, where you store files) and the paperclip icon 📎 (attaches files to a specific chat).

What to actually ask it

  • "Quiz me on this week's vocabulary."
  • "Summarize the key themes from this week's lectures."
  • "Create a practice test based on the rubric."
  • "Explain [concept] in simpler terms, then check whether I actually understood it by asking me to explain it back."

GroupChat

You can share a bot with classmates so a study group works off the same grounded tool instead of everyone re-explaining context to their own separate chat.
